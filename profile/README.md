## Overview
Locus Map for Windows brings together offline maps, GPS track recording, and a built-in locus map route planner so outdoor enthusiasts can prepare a trip before they ever leave the house. It's built around the idea that a good map should still work when there's no signal, which is why downloaded map tiles, saved tracks, and points of interest all stay available offline. Whether you're mapping out a weekend hike or a long cycling route, the app keeps planning, navigating, and reviewing your trip in one place.

## Features

## Getting Started
The first time Locus Map opens, it's worth spending a few minutes downloading the offline map for the region you'll be exploring, since everything else in the app builds on having that map ready locally. From there, dropping a few waypoints and sketching a route in the route planner gives you something to follow once you're outside, and turning on track recording before you set out means the trip gets logged automatically as you go.
